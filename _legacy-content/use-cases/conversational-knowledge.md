# UC6 — Conversational Knowledge Access

**"Ask your AI" as interface to structured knowledge.**

A knowledge document (or pod) serves as context for any AI. Users explore
interactively at their own level — from "explain it simply" to "show me a
SPARQL query."

## What it enables

- Websites that adapt to the visitor's expertise level
- Documentation that answers questions instead of presenting walls of text
- AI-powered exploration of any structured knowledge base

## Meta-demonstration

This chatbot is itself an example of UC6. You're talking to an AI that has
structured knowledge about sempods as its context. The knowledge lives in
markdown files today — in the future, it will live in a pod.

## Planned

Phase 1: Markdown knowledge files with AI prompt (current implementation).
Phase 2: Knowledge lives in a pod — the website eats its own dog food.
