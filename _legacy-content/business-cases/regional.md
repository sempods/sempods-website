# BC2 — Regional Knowledge Pod

**Archetype: Regional/Domain Monopoly**
An organization has exclusive domain knowledge — sempods turns it into a
subscription product.

## The Insight

A regional publisher's real asset is not articles — it's **structured regional
knowledge.** They know what was decided in every council session, why there's a
construction site on a street, which businesses opened, what events are happening.
This knowledge is locked in articles — unstructured, buried in archives, not
queryable, not linkable.

## The Vision

```
pod.publisher.example
    ├── /region/cityA/politics     (council decisions)
    ├── /region/cityA/events       (cultural calendar)
    ├── /region/cityA/traffic      (construction, road closures)
    ├── /region/cityA/economy      (businesses, jobs)
    ├── /region/cityA/sports       (local sports)
    ├── /subscriber/alice/ai-memory (Premium: personal AI context)
    └── /public/events             (free: event calendar as LOD)
```

## Subscription Model — Context-Based

| Tier | Access | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Public events | 0 EUR |
| **Basis** | All contexts, one city | 3-5 EUR/month |
| **Region** | All contexts, all sub-regions | 5-8 EUR/month |
| **Premium** | Everything + personal AI memory | 8-12 EUR/month |

The subscription is not "access to articles" — it's **access to regional
knowledge.** Articles are consumed once. Knowledge is queryable, combinable,
and grows over time.

## The Pitch

For young users: **"Ask your AI what's happening in your city"** instead of
"subscribe to our newspaper app." Completely different value proposition.

For the publisher: **Defensible position.** No Google, no ChatGPT knows what
was decided in yesterday's local council meeting. The regional knowledge
monopoly is real — a pod makes it accessible and monetizable.
